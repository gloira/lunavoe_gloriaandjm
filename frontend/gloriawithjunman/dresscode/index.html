Guest Guide

Dress Code

Formal / Semi-Formal · Daytime Ceremony

We kindly ask guests to dress in formal or semi-formal attire. Please avoid overly casual outfits so everyone looks polished in daylight photos.

Ladies

Elegant midi or long dresses, dressy jumpsuits or coordinated sets. Soft, flowy fabrics photograph beautifully.

Soft Blush Dusty Rose Butter Yellow Champagne Yellow

Please avoid white, ivory, or anything very close to bridal tones.

Gentlemen

Neat and polished looks are recommended. Jackets are optional.

  • White or light blue shirts
  • Dressy polos (white / beige / light blue / light grey)
  • Tailored trousers (beige, khaki, light brown, warm grey)

Please Avoid

  • T-shirts, tank tops
  • Shorts
  • Denim / ripped jeans
  • Flip-flops or casual sandals
  • Sportswear / beachwear
