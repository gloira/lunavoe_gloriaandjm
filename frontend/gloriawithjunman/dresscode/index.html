Guest Guide

Dress Code

Formal / Semi-Formal · Daytime Ceremony

We kindly ask guests to dress in formal or semi-formal attire. Please keep the look neat, polished, and wedding-appropriate for daylight photos.

Ladies

We recommend elegant midi or long dresses, dressy jumpsuits, or coordinated sets in soft, warm tones.

Neutrals

Recommended colours (best for daylight):

Soft Blush Dusty Rose Butter Yellow Champagne Yellow

Neutral options are also welcome: Sand, Taupe, Greige, Soft Champagne.

Please avoid white, ivory, and very light bridal tones.

Gentlemen

A clean, polished look is perfect — jackets are optional.

Polos & Linen

Jackets & Formal

Easy & safe options:

  • White or light blue shirt
  • Light linen shirt or a dressy polo (white / beige / light blue / light grey)
  • Tailored trousers (beige / khaki / light brown / warm grey)

Colour note (optional): Gentlemen may also wear the ladies' palette (blush / butter yellow / champagne) as a shirt or polo, paired with neutral trousers.

Please Avoid (Important)

To keep the ceremony atmosphere formal/semi-formal, kindly avoid overly casual outfits such as:

  • T-shirts / tank tops
  • Shorts
  • Denim / ripped jeans
  • Flip-flops or casual beach sandals
  • Sportswear / beachwear

Thank you for dressing up with us — we can't wait to celebrate together!
